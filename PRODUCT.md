# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Founders, small-business owners and teams who need software built and are deciding whether to hire Ridho (directly or through Erkabased) for a project.
- Engineering leads and recruiters checking his background, skills and track record.
- People who found him through his content (TikTok/Instagram @ridhobahastech, Medium, LinkedIn) and want to see who he is and what else he makes.

## Product Purpose

ridhokhalis.com is Ridho Khalis's personal site. It presents him as a professional software engineer who also creates content about building software, AI and automation. Success: a qualified visitor reaches out about a project.

## Positioning

An engineer who ships his own products end to end (Rekanka, Koogita) and runs a dev studio (Erkabased), while working full-time as a backend engineer at Transfez, and who explains what he learns in public. The work is the proof: real products, real systems, real posts.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from social bios, LinkedIn, Medium articles, the Erkabased site, or a shared link from Ridho.
- Primary action: contact Ridho about a project (email; Erkabased as the studio route).
- Secondary: read/watch his content, view his products and projects, download the resume.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: English (confirmed 2026-11-24). Some linked posts are in Indonesian; they stay as they are.
- No newsletter (confirmed 2026-11-24). No email lead capture.
- Stack: Astro 7 static site with Sass, deployed at https://ridhokhalis.com. GA4 via Partytown.
- Resume PDF at /resume.pdf.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work, not adjectives: every claim points to a product, system, number or post.
2. Contact is always one step away.
3. Warm and human first, technical depth one click deeper.
4. Professional enough for a CTO, approachable enough for a shop owner.
